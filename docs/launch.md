## Show HN
**Show HN: Atomberg Console, a menu bar app for Atomberg fans that works over your LAN**

Atomberg's developer API gives you 100 cloud calls a day. Their fans also broadcast state and accept commands
over UDP on your network, so this app controls fans directly and only uses the cloud as a fallback. It talks to
exactly one host and two UDP ports, and CI fails if that changes. Swift, no dependencies, source-available
(PolyForm Noncommercial). Unofficial, not affiliated with Atomberg.
